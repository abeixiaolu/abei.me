## home

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished desktop website concept screenshot for a personal website called ABEI'S DESK, belonging to 阿北, a frontend developer and designer in Hangzhou. Landscape 1536x1024 composition, edge-to-edge website screenshot, no browser chrome, no device mockup, no outer presentation board.
Art direction: Nintendo Famicom / NES nostalgic pixel-art desktop. Rich intentional pixel art with crisp visible stepped pixels and restrained limited colors, classic warm ivory #F7F0D8, Famicom red #D63732, dark ink #282627, muted sky blue #6AA8CB, forest green #438454, ochre yellow #E8B850. No gradients, no smooth 3D rendering, no blur, no glass effects. Sophisticated curated website with generous clean space, chunky 2px dark outlines, offset pixel shadows and squared corners. Pixel display font for Latin titles; beautiful readable Chinese text with a slightly pixel-like aesthetic. Small icons and artwork are pixelated, but text remains comfortably readable. This is a real navigable website, not a HUD-filled game or an illustration with random tiny labels. No Nintendo wordmark. Header at top about 80px tall, warm ivory, dark bottom border; left red square pixel monogram and bold ABEI'S DESK; right nav labels '首页', '博客', '项目', '实验', '装备', '相册', active item solid red with ivory letters. Small audio toggle at far right. Bottom thin ivory footer '© ABEI · HANGZHOU, CHINA' left and 'TAKE YOUR TIME.' right.
Recurring companion: recognizable small chibi pixel sprite of Inuyasha (犬夜叉), long silver-white hair, two white dog ears, red robe and hakama, barefoot, carrying the large ivory Tessaiga sword; cute but detailed classic game sprite around 90px tall. Companion must be visually integrated into the desktop and able to jump between pages. Do not add Mario or other characters.
Page: HOME, active '首页'. Large editorial page title above scene, left aligned '你好，我是阿北。' and below in readable smaller Chinese '写代码，做设计，偶尔带相机出门。'; right a small red outlined 'PLAYER 01' label and 3 tiny red hearts, not an excessive HUD.
Main view is a large exquisite side-on pixel-art studio desktop vignette filling central 65% of the image: warm ivory wall, window at upper right with blue sky pixel clouds and distant Hangzhou city silhouettes; bookshelf and pinned note at left; an old cream CRT monitor near center-left, a Famicom red-white console with controller on the oak desk, keyboard, small camera, a green plant at right. Desk physically spans whole middle width. CRT screen is blue with a cream inset displaying 'ABEI / OS' and 'PRESS START', and a highly legible red button '进入博客 →'. Everything in this room should be pixel art rather than realistic/isometric 3D. At right of CRT stand four beautifully drawn chunky cartridge-shaped desktop shortcuts in red, blue, green, yellow, labeled '博客', '项目', '实验', '相册'. They look like interactive website navigation objects, not arbitrary toys.
Inuyasha small sprite standing confidently on desk between screen and cartridges, sword over shoulder; a cream pixel speech bubble says '出发，去看看！'. A few restrained gold step particles near feet suggest motion. Small clear unobtrusive interaction instruction below desk: '点击卡带切换页面 · 点击犬夜叉互动'.
